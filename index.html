Talk 01 · Science

High Performance Genes

Most plans are built for the average person. You aren't one.

  • Why one-size plans stallThe same training, food and recovery can work for one person and fail the next.
  • What your genes can and can't tell youGenes are a blueprint and a starting point. They are not destiny, and the work is still yours.
  • From data to a daily planSimple moves for training, nutrition and recovery that fit how you were made.

For performance, wellness and longevity summits · coaches and trainers · executive health

Talk 02 · Faith, family and business

Anchored in Faith

What do you do when your life gets flipped upside down?

  • IdentityKnow who you are before the crisis tries to tell you. It is the one thing chaos can't take.
  • When you're staring at deathThe strategies that kept our family steady, and how we found calm in the chaos when my wife's life was on the line.
  • Light through your habitsDarkness is just the absence of light. The daily habits that bring it back, one day at a time.
"I used to pray these things would never happen. Now I'm grateful for the man I am because of them."Daniel

For leadership teams · churches and faith-based audiences · anyone walking through crisis
